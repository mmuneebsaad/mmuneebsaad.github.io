About

About

I'm a Senior Researcher in Generative AI in the Department of Computer Science at Munster Technological University (MTU), Cork, and part-time lecturer across the Departments of Computer Science and Mathematics. I'm affiliated with Lero — the Research Ireland Centre for Software — and ADAPT — the Research Ireland Centre for AI-Driven Digital Content Technology.

I hold a PhD in Computing from MTU (2025), on training challenges of Generative Adversarial Networks for biomedical image synthesis, supervised by Dr Ruairi O'Reilly and Dr Mubashir Husain Rehmani. Before that I completed an MSc in Electrical Engineering — Deep Learning and Time Series Analytics — at COMSATS University Islamabad, Pakistan.

My current work spans GAN architecture design and training stability, low-resource neural machine translation for Irish, and evaluation frameworks for generative model quality and diversity. I'm a contributor to the open-source adaptMLLM framework for Irish–English NMT.

Research

Research

GANs

Biomedical image synthesis

I work on GAN architecture design and training optimisation for X-ray and other medical imaging tasks. My contributions include MSG-SAGAN (a self-attention guided multi-scale gradient GAN), adaptive input-image normalisation for the mode collapse problem, and original early stopping criteria for training stability.

NMT

Low-resource neural machine translation

I fine-tune large language models and sequence-to-sequence architectures for Irish–English and English–Irish translation via the open-source adaptMLLM framework. My focus is on data scarcity, morphological complexity, and domain adaptation.

Eval

Generative model evaluation

I'm extending the Accessibility-Coverage Curve (ACCC) framework — originally proposed in the context of synthetic medical image assessment — into natural language generation and synthetic data quality measurement.
